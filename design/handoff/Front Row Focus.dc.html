1Front Row Focus — identity, screens, copy, motion
IDENTITY
Kept your four colours; tuned them so the amber is the only warm thing on screen. The follow-spot on stage is the lamp in the room — same hex, same falloff. Teal appears only when something is held. Red appears only on Hard, and only where the rope and ring are. Everything else is one purple-black, so the two scenes read as one night.
TYPE
Proposing Big Shoulders Display 800 for titles — a gig-poster grotesk with more shoulder than Barlow Condensed, holds at 56px on a 400px phone. Body is Public Sans: plain, calm, slightly governmental, which is exactly what a notification you didn't ask for sounds like. The Tweaks panel lets you swap the title face to Barlow Condensed or Anton to compare.
DECISIONS
Cold open, agreed: the stage and "THE DRUMS" are the title card. Room says one thing: rain, clock, cat and headphones are scenery only — no rings, no hit areas. Only the Walkman and the thoughts are targets. Ending is included and speaks plainly once, in three short lines, then offers to play it again. Haptic and sound notes sit under each board.
